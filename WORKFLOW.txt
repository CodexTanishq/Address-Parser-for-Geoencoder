1. CORE OBJECTIVE & ONE-LINE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For every customer address, predict:
  (1) A robust location pin (x, y) in local Cartesian metres.
  (2) A calibrated uncertainty radius r_alpha with an honest coverage guarantee
      (such as an 80% or 90% confidence region).

THE ONE-SENTENCE WORKFLOW IDEA:
Evidence -> Prior P(L) -> Trust (pi_i) + Likelihoods P(V_i|L) -> Bayesian Posterior P(L|E) 
         -> Pin (Mode/Medoid) + Raw Uncertainty -> Conformal Calibration (q_alpha) 
         -> Honest Pin + Calibrated Radius (R50, R90)

WHAT IS IN SCOPE:
- Location inference combining commercial geocoder, locality, landmarks, and GPS visits.
- Rigorous handling of the two operational regimes:
    * Branch A: Visited addresses (Bayesian grid fusion with robust likelihoods).
    * Branch B: No-visit / cold-start addresses (Convex Hull with fuzzy normal boundaries).
- Handling multiple visits, conflicting visits, and suspicious/adversarial visits.
- Comparison of all candidate spatial, clustering, Bayesian, and ML estimators.
- Conformal calibration against held-out ground truth to prevent overclaiming.

WHAT IS NOT IN SCOPE:
- Raw NLP address parsing, tokenization, or text embeddings (provided as structured clues
  by an upstream team via our formal ParsedAddressClues contract).

2. EMPIRICAL DATASET FACTS (FOUNDATIONAL REALITY)
================================================================================
Every modeling choice is grounded in 9 verified dataset facts:

[F1] Ground Truth Sample Size:
     Only 100 addresses have surveyed verified true coordinates.
     Official partition: 66 Train / 19 Validation / 15 Test.
     Implication: High-capacity end-to-end neural models will overfit; robust,
     semi-parametric Bayesian and geometric methods are required.

[F2] Commercial Geocoder Precision Variation:
     Median errors vary sharply by precision tier:
     - Street: ~109 m median error
     - Locality: ~386 m median error
     - Pincode: ~1,376 m median error
     - Rooftop: Only 1 surveyed instance in ground truth.
     Implication: Precision must dictate prior width sigma_prec; rooftop cannot be
     fit from n=1 and requires conservative fallback.

[F3] Locality Dominance:
     2,052 out of 2,880 catalog addresses (71.3%) have locality precision.
     Implication: The geocoder provides an area-level guess, not a door-level pin.

[F4] Cold-Start / No-Visit Prevalence:
     Among 100 surveyed truth addresses, 49 have visits and 51 do not (1,477 of 3,117
     in broader dataset).
     Implication: The no-visit case is a primary operating mode (51%), not an edge case.

[F5] Door-Reaching Outcomes are Powerful:
     Borrower met at home: ~34 m median error.
     Family met at home: ~16 m median error.
     Locked premises at door: ~20 m median error.
     Implication: High-confidence field visits provide vastly superior spatial signals
     compared to locality geocoders.

[F6] Heavy Tails & Outliers:
     Locked premises P90 reaches ~3.9 km; some borrower visits sit ~7.9 km away.
     Implication: Gaussian likelihoods fail in the tails; heavy-tailed (Student-t)
     or explicit outlier mixtures are mandatory.

[F7] Failed Searches as Negative Evidence:
     'address_not_traceable' has median distance of ~1,603 m from truth.
     Implication: Must be treated as search trails, not positive house observations.

[F8] Empirical Error-to-Accuracy Ratio (~2.4x):
     Median ratio of true door error to phone gps_accuracy_m is ~2.4x.
     Implication: Never set sigma_i = accuracy_i. Must scale: sigma_i = sqrt(a^2 + (b * acc)^2).

[F9] Visit Fraud & Suspicious Artifacts:
     172 visits share duplicate photo hashes across different addresses; agent FA009
     exhibits abnormal door-visit error.
     Implication: Model must compute an explicit visit trust score pi_i.

3. COORDINATE REFERENCE SYSTEM (CRS) & AUDIT PROOF
================================================================================
1. Topocentric Planar Cartesian System:
   - Origin: Town territory center at (0, 0).
   - Units: Metres (m).
   - Mathematical Proof: Observed coordinates range up to |x|, |y| ~ 3,900 m,
     which exceed geographic latitude limits [-90, 90] and longitude limits [-180, 180],
     and match authoritative town radii (3,800 m to 4,800 m) in towns.csv.
2. Distance Metric:
   - Straight-line Euclidean distance is exact:
     error = sqrt( (x_pred - x_true)^2 + (y_pred - y_true)^2 )
3. Scope Exclusions:
   - Financial collections logs (dial_attempts.csv, payments.csv, loan records)
     are strictly excluded from physical location estimation.


================================================================================
4. LEAK-FREE SPATIAL PARTITIONING
================================================================================
- To prevent spatial data leakage, addresses within a 50 m radius or sharing a
  spatial locality grid are hashed together into a spatial_cluster_id.
- Deterministic SHA-256 modulo hashing splits clusters:
    * 66% Train (Prior fitting & scale model training)
    * 19% Validation (Conformal quantile calibration)
    * 15% Test (Strict untouched evaluation)
- Ground-truth test surveyed coordinates are never accessed during training
  or calibration.

6. DETAILED STEP-BY-STEP MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS
================================================================================

STEP 1: Map Representation as Candidate Locations L
- Plausible map area divided into a 41x41 grid of discrete candidate cells L.
- Grid naturally represents non-Gaussian, multimodal, and irregular beliefs.

STEP 2: Geocoder Prior Model P(L)
- Centered at commercial geocoder point g = (g_x, g_y):
    P(L) proportional to exp( - ||L - g||^2 / (2 * sigma_prec^2) )
- Precision widths learned from training errors:
    * rooftop: sigma = 25.0 m (conservative fallback)
    * street: sigma = 100.0 m
    * locality: sigma = 330.0 m
    * pincode: sigma = 1,200.0 m

STEP 3 & 4: Visit Likelihood & Outlier Mixture Model
- Each check-in observation z_i is modeled as a mixture:
    p(z_i | L) = pi_i * InlierDensity(z_i; L, sigma_i) + (1 - pi_i) * Outlier(z_i)
- Inlier density options:
    * Gaussian: (1 / (2*pi*sigma_i^2)) * exp(-0.5 * ||z_i - L||^2 / sigma_i^2)
    * Student-t (nu = 3): (1 / (pi * nu * sigma_i^2)) * (1 + ||z_i - L||^2 / (nu * sigma_i^2))^(-(nu+2)/2)
- Outlier density: Uniform broad background over town area (rho = 1e-8).
- Visit trust score pi_i in [0, 1]:
    * Credibility derived from outcome, GPS accuracy, dwell time, photo hash reuse,
      and agent hotspot checks.
    * Meeting context: Home-confirmed visits receive positive weight; work/road
      meetings receive pi = 0.0; failed searches are treated as search trails.
    * Single-agent capping: Multi-visits by same agent discounted by min(1, 1.5/sqrt(k)).

STEP 5: Learned Visit Uncertainty sigma_i
- Physical scatter model:
    sigma_i = sqrt( a^2 + (b * accuracy_i)^2 )
    Parameters fitted: a = 8.0 m (unavoidable door scatter floor), b = 2.4 (empirical ratio).

STEP 6 & 7: Bayesian Posterior Fusion & Grid Point Estimation
- Joint posterior over grid:
    P(L | E) proportional to P(L) * Product_{i=1}^N p(z_i | L)
- Point Estimates:
    * Posterior Mode (MAP): Cell with maximum posterior probability mass.
    * Posterior Medoid: Candidate point minimizing expected Euclidean distance to posterior.

STEP 8: Robust Heavy-Tailed Likelihoods
- Student-t density provides polynomial (rather than exponential) decay in tails,
  preventing large valid check-ins from being excessively penalized while keeping
  resilience against outliers.

STEP 9: Alternative Robust & Spatial Estimators
- Coordinate Median: (median(x), median(y))
- Weighted Geometric Median: Weiszfeld L1-norm minimizer argmin sum w_i ||x - z_i||.
- Robust Huber Center: Iteratively Reweighted Least Squares (IRLS) M-estimator
  with threshold delta = 25 m.
- DBSCAN Clustering: eps = 50 m, min_samples = 2 to isolate rogue outlier points.

STEP 10: Geocoder Residual ML (g + Delta) & Pseudo-Labelling
- Treats commercial geocoder as baseline g and trains HuberRegressor to predict
  shift (Delta_x, Delta_y) from spatial context features:
    x_pred = g_x + Delta_x
    y_pred = g_y + Delta_y
- Pseudo-labels: Augmented using unlabelled addresses having >= 2 concordant home
  visits (dispersion <= 30 m, trust >= 0.75).

STEP 11 & 12: No-Visit Branch: Convex Hull + Fuzzy Normal Boundary
- Assembles evidence points P1, P2, ..., Pn with individual uncertainties sigma_1, ..., sigma_n:
    * Geocoder point g (sigma_prec)
    * Locality centroid (sigma_locality = 350 m)
    * Landmark / POI (sigma_poi = 150 m)
- Convex Hull computed via Quickhull algorithm.
- For each edge between vertex A and vertex B:
    * sigma_edge = (sigma_A + sigma_B) / 2
    * Outward perpendicular shift: D_edge = z_alpha * sigma_edge
      (z_50 = 0.674, z_80 = 0.842, z_90 = 1.282)
- Shifted edges intersect to construct the 'Confidence Hull'.
- Predicted Pin: Hull Centroid.
- Raw Radius: R_alpha = max distance from hull centroid to any Confidence Hull vertex.
- Fallbacks:
    * 1 point: returns geocoder point with sigma_prec directly.
    * 2 points: returns segment midpoint with half-length + z * avg_sigma.

STEP 13: Split-Conformal Calibration
- Calibration set: 19 held-out validation addresses.
- Nonconformity scores:
    score_j = error_j / raw_radius_j
- Conformal multiplier q_alpha: Empirical (1 - alpha) quantile of calibration scores.
- Final Calibrated Radius:
    r_final = q_alpha * raw_radius
- Group-Aware (Mondrian) Conformal: Evaluates visited and no-visit cohorts separately
  to guarantee honest empirical coverage across both regimes.

STEP 14: Operational Decision Logic
- 'direct_dispatch': Tight radius (R90 <= 150 m) and high-confidence home visit (trust >= 0.65).
- 'plan_visit': Moderate radius (R90 <= 250 m) or reliable street/rooftop geocoder without visits.
- 'verify_first': Large radius (R90 > 250 m) or unvisited coarse locality/pincode geocoder.
- 'revisit_needed': Contradictory visit check-ins (>80 m apart) or reused photo hashes.
